## Survey: VNShop Mobile Shopping Behavior

### Share this link after creating the form:

```
https://forms.google.com/YOUR_FORM_ID
```

### Questions (7 questions, ~3 min to complete):

1. **How often do you shop on your mobile phone?** *(Single select)*
   - Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Rarely

2. **What payment methods do you use most?** *(Multi select)*
   - COD (Cash on Delivery) / MoMo / VNPay/ZaloPay / Bank transfer / Credit card

3. **What makes you abandon a shopping cart?** *(Multi select)*
   - High shipping costs / Complex checkout / Slow app / Lost connection / Wanted to compare prices / Changed my mind

4. **How would you rate your home internet/mobile connection?** *(Single select)*
   - Excellent / Good / Poor / Very poor

5. **If an app could save your cart when offline, how likely would you use it?** *(Scale 1-5)*
   - 1 (Not useful) → 5 (Very useful)

6. **How do you feel about push notifications from shopping apps?** *(Single select)*
   - Love them / Okay if relevant / Only order updates / Only promotions / I disable all

7. **What features would make you choose this app over others?** *(Open text)*
